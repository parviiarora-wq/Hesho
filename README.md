# hesho

**Show up. Leave proof.**

hesho is a social wellness app built around a simple idea: show up and leave proof.

Instead of being another fitness tracker, hesho makes health and wellness more social. You can share your progress, discover communities and challenges, track your own journey, and find people to actually play sports with.

I wanted the app to feel more like a social platform than a traditional health app, so the focus is on people, interaction, discovery and actually doing things together.

## What is in hesho

- **Home**: social feed, stories, likes, comments, saves, shares and progress posts
- **Discover**: challenges, communities and people
- **Journey**: personal progress, consistency and milestones
- **Profile**: public social identity, posts, progress and challenges
- **Play**: find people nearby who play the same sport and are at a similar level
- **Themes**: Midnight, Volt, Ocean and Mono

## Product idea

The main parts of the app have different jobs:

- **Home** is where you see what people are doing.
- **Discover** is where you find something worth doing.
- **Journey** is where you see your own progress.
- **Profile** is your public identity on hesho.
- **Play** is for finding someone to actually play with.

## Current version

This repository contains the current frontend prototype.

The current version uses mock data and frontend interactions. It does not have a real backend, database or production authentication yet.
